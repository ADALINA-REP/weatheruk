# Privacy Policy for Weather & Radar Applications

**Last Updated:** September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all weather applications developed under the **ADALINA / SISCOM** portfolio, including **WeatherUK**, **SkyCast**, **MeteoEspagne**, and related regional weather tracking applications (collectively referred to as "the Applications" or "our Services").

Your privacy is paramount to us. This document outlines how data is handled when you use our weather applications.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Location Data
- **Functionality:** Our Applications may request access to your device's location (GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular network data) solely to provide hyper-local weather forecasts, severe weather alerts, and regional rain radar maps.
- **Control:** Location access is strictly optional. You can grant or revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings. If location permissions are disabled, you can manually search for and select cities or postal regions.
- **Storage:** Location coordinates are used in real-time to fetch weather data from API endpoints and are **never sold or permanently stored** on external servers.

### B. Device & Usage Technical Data
- Non-identifiable technical data (such as device model, operating system version, app language, and performance metrics) may be processed to ensure stability, optimize layout rendering, and debug crashes.

---

## 2. Third-Party API Services & SDKs

To deliver accurate weather predictions, radar imagery, and app monetization, our Applications utilize trusted industry-standard third-party providers:

- **Weather Data Providers:** Open-Meteo API, RainViewer Radar, Sunrise-Sunset API. (These APIs process location parameters to return meteorological data without collecting personal identities).
- **Ad Networks & Analytics:** Google AdMob / Firebase Analytics (used for non-personalized app analytics and advertisement delivery in accordance with Google and Apple privacy policies).

---

## 3. Data Protection and Security

- **No Personal Data Sales:** We do not collect, trade, rent, or sell your personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to third-party data brokers.
- **Encryption:** All network requests to weather API services and backend servers are transmitted securely using industry-standard **HTTPS/TLS encryption**.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our Applications do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove such data.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Options

Under applicable privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA):
- You can enable or disable location services in your iOS or Android Settings.
- You can reset your device's Advertising Identifier via system settings.
