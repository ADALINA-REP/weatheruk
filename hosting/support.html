Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
🇪🇸 Tiempo España: ¿Cómo activar la localización y el radar en vivo?
En tu iPhone o teléfono Android, abre Ajustes ➔ Tiempo España ➔ Localización y elige "Al usar la app". La aplicación centrará automáticamente el radar de lluvia y los pronósticos en tu municipio.
🌧️ Why isn't the rain radar map updating?
Radar maps require an active internet connection to load tiles. Check that cellular data or Wi-Fi is enabled. If you use an ad-blocker or VPN, ensure it does not block map tile CDN endpoints.
📍 How do I search and save favorite locations?
Tap the Search / Cities tab in the navigation bar, search for any city, town, or province across Spain, the UK, the US, or France, and tap the heart icon to pin it to your dashboard.
🔔 How do I enable severe weather notifications?
Make sure Notifications are enabled in your device system settings for the application. Our weather apps alert you when high precipitation, storms, or severe weather conditions are detected in your area.
⛽ FuelSense: How are fuel prices refreshed?
FuelSense synchronizes daily with official governmental open-data repositories. Stations are filtered by proximity and price per liter for Diesel, SP95, SP98, E85, and LPG.
🗑️ How can I request data or cache deletion?
Our applications do not require user accounts. To delete locally stored cached preferences and favorites, simply clear the app cache in device settings or uninstall the app. For any privacy requests, you can email our support team directly.
