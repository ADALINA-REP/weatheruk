Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs all applications developed by ADALINA / SISCOM, including WeatherUK, FuelSense, SkyCast, MeteoEspagne, and related applications published under our Apple Developer and Google Play accounts (collectively, "the Applications" or "our Services").

Core Privacy Guarantee: We value your privacy above all else. We do not collect, trade, rent, or sell your personal identity or information to third-party data brokers.

1. Information Collection & Usage

A. Location Services Data

Our Applications may request access to location data (GPS, cellular networks, or Wi-Fi positioning) to deliver essential app features, such as hyper-local weather predictions, severe storm alerts, rain radar maps, and nearby fuel price comparison.

  • Location access is entirely optional and permission-based.
  • You can enable or disable location permissions at any time via your device settings.
  • Location coordinates are transmitted securely via HTTPS to query API endpoints in real-time and are never stored permanently on external servers.

B. Technical & Device Metrics

Non-identifiable technical diagnostic data (such as OS version, device model, app crash logs, and display parameters) may be processed to ensure app stability and optimize performance.

2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To provide accurate forecasts, map rendering, and app functionality, our Applications utilize trusted industry-standard SDKs and APIs:

  • Weather & Map Providers: Open-Meteo API, RainViewer Radar, Sunrise-Sunset API.
  • Monetization & Diagnostics: Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics (operating strictly under Apple App Store and Google Play privacy guidelines).

3. Data Protection & Encryption

All communication between our Applications and backend services is protected using HTTPS/TLS 1.3 protocol encryption to prevent unauthorized access or data interception.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Applications are designed for general audiences and do not knowingly collect or store personal data from children under 13 years of age.
