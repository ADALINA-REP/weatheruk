# Weather & Radar Applications — Support & Help Center

Welcome to the Official Support & Help Center for **ADALINA / SISCOM Weather Applications** (including **WeatherUK**, **SkyCast**, **MeteoEspagne**, and regional weather tracking apps).

---

## 🌧️ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I enable hyper-local weather alerts for my current location?
- Open your device's **Settings** app.
- Scroll down and select your weather app (**WeatherUK** / **SkyCast**).
- Tap **Location** and select **While Using the App**.
- Enable **Notifications** to receive severe rain and storm alerts.

### 2. Why isn't the rain radar map loading?
- Check your internet or Wi-Fi connection. Radar tiles require an active data connection.
- If you are using a VPN or ad-blocker, verify that network calls to RainViewer weather servers are allowed.

### 3. How do I add or change favorite cities?
- Open the app menu and navigate to **Cities** or **Search**.
- Type any city or postal code nationwide and tap **Add to Favorites**.
