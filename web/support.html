Frequently Asked Questions
🌧️ How do I enable live rain radar and location tracking?
Go to your device's Settings ➔ WeatherUK ➔ Location and set it to "While Using the App". Make sure Notification permissions are turned on for storm warnings.
⚡ Why isn't the radar map updating?
Verify that your device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection. If you use a VPN or custom firewall, ensure calls to RainViewer weather endpoints are permitted.
📍 How do I add or manage favorite cities?
Open the main menu, select Search / Cities, type any UK or global location, and tap the heart icon to save it to your home dashboard.
