Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs all weather applications developed under the ADALINA / SISCOM portfolio, including WeatherUK, SkyCast, MeteoEspagne, and regional weather tracking applications (collectively, "the Applications" or "our Services").

Core Privacy Commitment: We respect your privacy. We do not collect, rent, or sell your personal information (such as your name, address, or email) to data brokers.

1. Information Collection & Use

A. Precise & Approximate Location Data

Our Applications utilize location coordinates (GPS, cellular, or Wi-Fi data) solely to deliver real-time hyper-local weather predictions, severe storm alerts, and regional rain radar maps.

  • Location access is 100% optional. You may grant or revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings.
  • If location permissions are disabled, you can manually search for any city or postal region worldwide.
  • Location data is processed ephemerally for weather API queries and is never stored permanently on external servers.

B. Technical Device Metrics

Anonymized technical data (such as OS version, device model, app performance logs, and language preference) may be collected to prevent crashes and optimize application performance.

2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

To provide reliable meteorological forecasts and app functionality, the Applications integrate with trusted service providers:

  • Meteorological APIs: Open-Meteo, RainViewer Radar, and Sunrise-Sunset API.
  • Monetization & Analytics: Google AdMob & Firebase (delivering non-personalized ads and crash diagnostics in accordance with Apple App Store policies).

3. Data Security & Encryption

All network traffic between the Applications and meteorological data providers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS 1.3 protocols to prevent interception.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Applications do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted data, please contact us for immediate deletion.

5. Your Choices & Controls

  • Location Access: Toggle on/off under Settings ➔ Privacy ➔ Location Services on iOS or Android.
  • Ad Identifiers: Reset or disable advertising tracking via system settings.
